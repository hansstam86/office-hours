How it works

1.Pick a city where I'll be, and tell me about your project in a few lines.
2.If it's a fit, I'll pick a time and we'll meet at a café near you. You buy the coffee (or lunch).
3.45–60 minutes. You talk, I ask annoying questions, you leave with blunt, practical feedback.

Where I'll be

Cities and dates change. Not in the list? Ask anyway: I'm in Stockholm most months and in Shenzhen every few months. In Berlin I only meet in Schmargendorf.

💻 Online works too, on Google Meet. But I strongly prefer meeting in person: a café table and a prototype you can put in my hands beat a video call every time. Choose online only if we really can't be in the same city.

Good topics

  • You have a physical product idea and want to know if it's worth your next year.
  • You're about to spend real money: tooling, a factory, a first batch, a Kickstarter.
  • Your factory in China says "very easy, no problem" and you don't believe them.
  • Cost, DFM, certification, EVT/DVT/PVT, finding the right supplier, moving production to Europe.

Not a fit: pitching me something to buy, recruiting, or "can you build this for free". I'll be honest if I can't help.
