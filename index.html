How it works

1.Pick a city where I'll be, and tell me in a line or two what you'd like to talk about.
2.If it's a fit, I'll reply with a time and a café (in Berlin: always in Schmargendorf). You buy the coffee (or lunch).
3.45–60 minutes of honest conversation. If you bring a project, you'll get blunt, practical feedback.

Where I'll be

Cities and dates change. Not in the list? Ask anyway: I'm in Stockholm most months and in Shenzhen every few months. In Berlin I only meet in Schmargendorf.

💻 Online works too, on Google Meet. But I strongly prefer meeting in person: a café table and a prototype you can put in my hands beat a video call every time. Choose online only if we really can't be in the same city.

What can we talk about?

Anything, really. Some things I know a lot about:

  • Hardware & factories: is your product idea worth your next year, what it really costs to make, DFM, EVT/DVT/PVT, the factory that says "very easy, no problem".
  • Supply chains: finding and managing suppliers, sourcing, moving production to Europe.
  • China: 20 years of working there, Shenzhen, how business really works, what's changing.
  • Business models & startups: your idea, your pricing, your first customers, whatever keeps you up at night.
  • Anything else you think makes for a good conversation.

The only thing I'm not up for is a sales pitch. I just want to have good conversations with good people.
